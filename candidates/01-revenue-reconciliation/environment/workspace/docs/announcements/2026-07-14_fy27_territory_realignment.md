# FY27 territory realignment

From: Sales Operations - 2026-07-14

The FY27 territory plan takes effect **1 August 2026**. Roughly 30% of accounts
move to a new account owner as territories are rebalanced across NA and EMEA.

- CRM will write a new account record version effective 2026-08-01 for every
  reassigned account (previous version end-dated 2026-07-31). Account ids do not
  change.
- No change to segments, regions, billing accounts, contracts or invoicing.
- Commission and pipeline reports switch to the new owners from August.
