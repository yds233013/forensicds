# Growth plan list price update

From: Pricing & Packaging - 2026-07-21

The list price of the **Growth (monthly, per seat)** plan changes from **$45.00 to
$49.00 per seat per month**, effective for invoices dated on or after
**1 August 2026**. Customers on contract pricing are not affected until renewal.

Billing will publish plan version 2 of `GROWTH_M` in the plan catalogue. Expect
recognized revenue for list-priced Growth customers to rise ~9% month over month.
