# INC-2291 customer communication and SLA credits

From: Customer Success - 2026-08-21

On **14 August 2026** a regional outage (INC-2291, 3h12m) breached the uptime SLA
for a number of customers. Affected customers receive an **SLA credit of 10% of
one month's subscription fee**.

- Billing issues the credits as credit notes (`reason_code = sla_credit`) between
  20 and 27 August against each customer's main subscription line.
- Credits reduce August recognized revenue (policy section 4).
- Postmortem: PM-2291 (internal).
