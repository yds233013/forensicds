# CRM contract types (Sales Ops)

`contracts.contract_type` is chosen by the deal desk when a contract is booked, for commission and pipeline
reporting: `new_business`, `renewal`, `upsell`, `downsell`. Win-backs of former customers are booked as
`new_business` or `renewal` depending on the rep's comp plan. Reps booking renewals they did not originate sometimes
book them as `new_business` for commission purposes; the March 2026 Sales reorg re-mapped renewal owners again.
