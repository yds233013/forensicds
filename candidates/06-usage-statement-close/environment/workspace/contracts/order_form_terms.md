# Cobalt Data Platform: Order Form, Usage Terms (standard excerpt)

*Applies to all enterprise orders signed on or after 2025-01-01. Commercial parameters (included units, tier
quantities and rates) are set per order and recorded in the rate card.*

**4. Usage fees**

4.1 Usage is measured by Cobalt's metering service in 4-hour windows and attributed to the calendar month (UTC) in
which it occurred.

4.2 Each order includes a monthly allowance of included units per meter. The allowance applies to each calendar month
separately and does not carry over. Usage above the allowance in a month is charged at the overage rates in effect for
that month: the Tier 1 rate up to the Tier 1 quantity, the Tier 2 rate above it.

4.3 Cobalt issues a usage statement for each month after the month's statement close (see Billing Schedule). Each
statement reflects the usage reported to Cobalt's metering service by the statement close.

4.4 Usage reported after the statement close for the month in which it occurred, and corrections to usage already
invoiced, are invoiced or credited on a subsequent statement. Issued statements are final and are not re-issued.

**Billing Schedule.** The statement close for a month is 00:00 UTC on the fourth day of the following month.
