# Finance glossary (Store Investments, extract)

**Uplift.** The change a programme causes in a store's figure: actual, minus what the store would have done over the
same weeks without the programme. For sales figures, uplift is expressed in log points (0.025 ≈ 2.5%).

**Run-rate.** Performance once a change has bedded in: weeks 13 to 26 after go-live, counting the go-live week as
week 1. Weeks that are not comparable trading weeks (`docs/kpi_handbook.md`) are left out.

**Store-level vs programme-level figures.** A store's run-rate uplift is the average over its run-rate weeks. A
programme-level or wave-level figure is the simple average across the stores concerned (each store counts once,
whatever its size), because capital is committed per store.

**Go-live.** The first trading week in which the installed equipment is in customer use.

**Tranche.** A tranche of a staged programme is a set of waves released together.
