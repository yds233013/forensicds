# SCO 2.0 business case (approved 2024-11-26, Capital Committee)

Sponsor: COO. Finance partner: FP&A (Store Investments).

## 1. What we are buying

SCO 2.0 replaces the self-checkout bank with the new scale-integrated units and, where the building allows it, a
big-basket self-checkout lane. Installation is staged in six waves (plan `rollout_plan`, version 2024-11).

| Kit | Capex per store | Maintenance per year |
|---|---|---|
| Full (with big-basket lane) | £410k | £22k |
| Compact | £265k | £15k |

## 2. Why

Store Operations expects faster checkout and larger consolidated shops. The Programme team tracks basket size and SCO
share as its programme KPIs.

## 3. Staged capital release and continuation gate

Capital is released in two tranches.

- **Tranche 1 (waves 1–4)** was released at approval.
- **Tranche 2 (waves 5–6)** is released only if the continuation gate is met: the expected **run-rate uplift in
  weekly net sales for the stores in waves 5–6, as planned**, is at least **2.5%**. At 2.5% the tranche pays back
  inside the kit's depreciation life at the planned kit mix; below it, it does not.

The gate figure is prepared by FP&A from the waves already live and presented to the Capital Committee with its
uncertainty. Definitions follow the finance glossary (`docs/finance/glossary.md`).

## 4. Out of scope

Reverting stores already installed is not considered (the capex is sunk and the kits have no resale value).
