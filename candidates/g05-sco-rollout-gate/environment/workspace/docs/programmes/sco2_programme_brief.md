# SCO 2.0 programme brief

Owner: Store Transformation (Programme team). Last updated 2026-09-01.

## The change

- New self-checkout units with integrated scales and faster card readers (all stores).
- **Big-basket lane**: a self-checkout lane sized for trolley shops, with a bagging area behind the tills. It needs a
  usable rear bagging bay; the store layout survey (June 2024) recorded which stores have one.
- Stores with a usable bay get the **full kit** (new units + big-basket lane). Stores without one get the **compact kit**
  (new units only). The kit is fixed when the store is scheduled and recorded on the work order.
- The software, screens and staffing model are identical in both kits.

## Installation

1. **Prewire** (overnight, store open) about two to three weeks before go-live.
2. **Install**: the old bank is removed and the new units and lanes fitted. The store closes for one or two trading
   days in the week before go-live or the one before that, depending on crew scheduling; larger jobs are split
   across both weeks.
3. **Go-live** on a Monday: the new equipment is in customer use from opening. Nothing is visible to customers before
   go-live.

Crews work to the wave plan, but dates move: supply and crew availability push a share of stores back one to four
weeks. The install log (`install_log`) records what actually happened; the plan is not updated after release.

## Assumptions stated at approval

- Stores are at least six miles from their nearest sister store; shoppers lost to a store are assumed to go to
  competitors or online, not to another Meridian store.
