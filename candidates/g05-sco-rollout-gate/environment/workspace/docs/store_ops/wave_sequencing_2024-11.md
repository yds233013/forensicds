# SCO 2.0 wave sequencing (Store Operations, November 2024)

How the store list was split into waves for plan version 2024-11.

1. **Readiness first.** Waves 1–2 are weighted towards Supercentres: they have the floor space, the longest staffed
   queues and dedicated install crews in every region.
2. **Within a format, stores with a usable rear bagging bay go earlier.** The big-basket lane is the headline feature
   and those installs are the ones the vendor wanted to prove first; stores without a bay were pushed to later waves.
3. **Crew geography.** Within those groups, stores were allocated to waves by installer region so that a crew works
   through neighbouring stores in sequence.
4. Waves 5–6 hold the remaining stores.

No store was chosen or deferred because of its sales performance.
