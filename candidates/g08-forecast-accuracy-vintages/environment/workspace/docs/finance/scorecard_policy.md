# Trading desk scorecard policy (extract)

Owner: Finance Business Partnering, Trading.

- The desk scorecard is compiled monthly from the imbalance cost report and the forecast accuracy pack.
- A scorecard month is final once the forecast accuracy pack for that delivery month is signed off. Scorecard months
  are not restated for subsequent settlement activity; the annual reconciliation review covers it.
- Scorecard figures feed the desk's annual objectives and the model governance board.
