# Portfolio restructures, 2026

Owner: Commercial Strategy, with Trading Operations.

## March: BUSINESS split into SME and IC

From delivery day **2 March 2026** the BUSINESS portfolio is replaced by two portfolios with separate hedging
strategies:
- **SME**: small business, both metering types, plus unmetered supplies;
- **IC**: industrial and commercial half-hourly.

The forecasting system produces SME and IC forecasts for delivery days from 2 March; BUSINESS forecasts stop at
1 March.

## June: unmetered supplies move to IC

From delivery day **1 June 2026** unmetered supplies (street lighting and signage contracts) are managed with the IC
book, because the contracts are negotiated by the corporate team.

Portfolio definitions over time are in `portfolio_membership`.
