# Demand definitions (Planning and Demand Science)

- **Sales:** units scanned at the tills.
- **Unconstrained demand:** units customers would buy if the item were on the shelf whenever the store is trading.
  This is what forecasts and buys are meant to cover.
- **Lost sales:** unconstrained demand that was not sold because the item was not on the shelf.
- **For a day that has already happened,** unconstrained demand is not directly observable when the shelf was empty
  for part of the day. The demand history records the expected value given what was observed that day.
- **When the item was on the shelf for all of the store's trading hours,** the day's sales are its demand.
- **Unit of purchase:** shoppers take one unit of an item per visit, and each sale is one unit
  (`docs/data/data_dictionary.md`).
