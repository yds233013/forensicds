# Real-distribution provenance — g24 (home-row policy value)

1. **Professional role.** Personalisation analyst deciding which ranker serves a high-traffic surface.
2. **Industry.** Consumer internet / e-commerce personalisation.
3. **Business decision.** What serves the home row next quarter — v6, v7 or v7_pd.
4. **Realistic inherited artifacts.** Logged impression/click/interaction data from the currently
   deployed policy, model score snapshots for the candidate policies, the offline gate report that
   scored v7 above v6 and v7_pd higher still, the gate code, and the surface's business metric
   definition.
5. **Statistical/ML problem.** Off-policy evaluation. The logs were generated by the deployed logging
   policy, so an offline metric computed on them without correcting for the exposure process estimates
   the wrong quantity; the ranking that wins offline need not be the one that wins online.
6. **Why this happens in real organizations.** Offline evaluation is an imperfect proxy for online
   performance because recommenders are interactive, and the correlation between offline and online
   outcomes is often weak. Recommendation data are biased by the exposure process, so offline
   objectives must correct for the policy that generated the data; OPE re-weights logged samples toward
   the target policy's distribution. Published work documents that flaws in offline evaluation setups
   are widespread rather than exceptional.
7. **Public sources.**
   - *Widespread Flaws in Offline Evaluation of Recommender Systems* (RecSys '23): https://doi.org/10.1145/3604915.3608839 · https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.14951
   - *Offline A/B Testing for Recommender Systems* (WSDM '18): https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3159652.3159687 · https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.07030
   - *A Survey on Causal Inference for Recommendation*: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.11666
8. **What is synthetic.** The platform, catalogue, users, logging policy and candidate policies.
9. **What is preserved.** A logged-bandit feedback structure with a known logging policy, a
   plausible offline gate that ranks the candidates confidently, and a decision that depends on the
   policy value rather than on a ranking metric.
10. **Why it belongs.** It is the suite's off-policy-evaluation task and the closest existing coverage
    of the delayed/selective-label blueprint: the outcome is observed only for what the logging policy
    chose to show.
