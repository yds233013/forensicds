# Aftermarket planning memo — FY27 critical-assembly build and field-service cover

From: Aftermarket Planning
To: Director, Reliability & Aftermarket

## What we are deciding

We commit the FY27 build quantity for spare critical assemblies and the field-service headcount
that fits emergency callouts. Both are sized on **how many units in today's installed base will
actually suffer an unplanned failure of their original assembly within 36 months of service age**,
because that is what generates an emergency assembly draw and a callout. Units whose assembly is
exchanged at scheduled overhaul before that happens are supplied from the planned-overhaul build,
which is a separate line; units retired from the fleet generate nothing.

## Procurement rule (unchanged since FY24)

> If the 36-month unplanned-failure rate on the installed base exceeds **28%**, we move to the
> expanded build and the higher cover level. At or below 28% we hold the baseline build.

## Where we are

`reports/installed_base_reliability_2026Q2.md` puts the figure at **55.2%**, which is well past the
trigger, and on that basis we have provisionally asked for the expanded build.

Operations have pushed back hard. Their argument is that most of our assemblies are exchanged on
the 24-month overhaul or leave the fleet before they ever fail in service, so a figure above half
cannot be what we will actually pay for. They are asking where the 55% comes from.

Engineering are looking at the same extract for a different purpose (see their note) and are
comfortable with a number in that range, which has not helped the discussion.

We need the number the procurement rule is actually defined on, and the recommendation that
follows from it.
