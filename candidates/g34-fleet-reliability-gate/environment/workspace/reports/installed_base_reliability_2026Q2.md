# Installed-base reliability — 2026 Q2 extract

Prepared by Aftermarket Analytics. Method: `fleet_reliability` 3.4.2, service-life estimation from
commissioning, units followed until their service record ends.

| Quantity | 36-month figure |
|---|---|
| Unplanned failure of the critical assembly | **55.2%** |
| Scheduled overhaul | 78.4% |
| Retirement | 24.1% |

Against the FY27 procurement rule (trigger 28%) the unplanned-failure figure clears the trigger
comfortably and we recommend the **expanded** build and cover level.

Caveats carried from the 2024 review: service records are incomplete for units still running at the
cut-off, which the life-table handles; the figures are for the installed base as a whole and are not
broken out by model or site.
