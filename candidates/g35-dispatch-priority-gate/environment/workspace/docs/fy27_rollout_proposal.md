# FY27 rollout proposal - Priority Dispatch

**From:** Merchant Growth
**To:** Marketplace Operations, Finance

## Proposal

Make Priority Dispatch **available to every merchant on the platform** from the start of FY27. No
tiering, no paid upgrade, no eligibility rule: every merchant gets the option, and activates it if
they want it.

## The case

The pilot readout shows priority merchants fulfilling substantially more of their demand. Applied
across the estate, Merchant Growth model this as the single largest available fulfilment improvement
this year, ahead of courier-supply incentives.

## The commitment gate

Finance will fund the FY27 build **only if the fulfilment improvement under the proposed rollout is at
least +1.5 percentage points.** Below that the engineering and support cost is not recovered and the
recommendation is to hold.

The number Finance needs is the improvement **once the rollout has happened** - that is, with the
feature available to every merchant, and with merchants activating it at whatever rate they choose.
Not the gap between merchants who had it and merchants who did not during a pilot.

## Open objection - Courier Operations

> Couriers are a fixed pool in a city on a day. We did not add couriers for this pilot. If a priority
> store is getting more of its orders out, someone else in that city is getting fewer of theirs out. I
> want to know what is left once everybody has it.
