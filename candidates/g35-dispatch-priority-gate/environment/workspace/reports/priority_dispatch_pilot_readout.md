# Priority Dispatch - pilot readout

**Prepared by:** Merchant Growth Analytics
**Method:** demand-weighted fulfilment of merchants assigned priority, minus merchants not assigned
priority, pooled across the whole pilot.

## Headline

Priority merchants fulfilled **+16.6 fulfilment points** more of their demand than non-priority
merchants over the 28-day pilot.

## Method notes

- Demand weighted, so a large store counts more than a small one.
- Pooled across all dispatch pools and all service dates in the window.
- Assignment was randomised, and balance checks on city, weekday, merchant size and pre-pilot
  fulfilment all pass.

## Read

The effect is large, consistent across cities, and comfortably clears the +1.5 point commitment gate.
Merchant Growth recommend proceeding with the FY27 rollout.
