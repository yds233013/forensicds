# FY27 residential capacity position

**From:** Resource Planning
**To:** Rates, Load Research, Executive

## The position

We hold **1,900 MW** of firm capacity for the residential block, contracted in 2024 against the peak
we were serving at the time. The regulator requires a **10 % planning reserve margin**, and we serve
**565,000** residential customers.

That gives a ceiling on mean peak-window demand of

```
1,900 MW / 1.10 / 565,000 customers = 3.057 kW per customer
```

**If the forecast mean peak-window load for the 2027 season is at or above 3.057 kW, we must procure
additional capacity.** Below it, we defer.

## Why this is live

The 2027 season is forecast materially warmer than our historical seasons. On the flat tariff our
own models put next season's mean peak-window load above the ceiling. The mandatory time-of-use
tariff is the mitigation: Rates expect customers to move discretionary load out of the peak window.

The question for this readout is simply whether that shift is large enough, **once every customer is
on the tariff**, to bring us back under 3.057 kW.

## What we need

The expected mean peak-window load per residential customer for the 2027 season, with the whole
estate on the time-of-use tariff, under the issued weather outlook - and the procurement call that
follows.

Procurement lead time is nine months. This decision is made now, on forecast.
