# G42 — trailing-twelve-month recordable incident rate (contractor safety rate)

An industrial services contractor must tell a client operator whether its recordable incident rate
breaches the access limit of 1.50 per 200,000 hours worked. The published report says 1.26 and
declares the company compliant; the contract rate is 1.72 and it is not.

Neither side of the ratio exists in the warehouse as a column. Hours worked have to be separated from
paid non-work time in the shift entries; recordable cases have to be separated from first aid and from
cases still under review, counted one per injured person rather than one per event, and placed in the
window by when the incident occurred rather than when it was logged. Agency workers belong in both the
numerator and the denominator. The company figure is the pooled rate, not the average of site rates,
and hours follow the site worked while cases follow the site of the incident.

Design, wrong-object panel and gate record: `research/g42/`.
