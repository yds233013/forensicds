# Fraud operations notes — quarter end

**Queue.** The quality sample is the only reason we can say anything about what the screen passes. It
is small: one passed transaction in N, and most of those come back legitimate, as they should.

**Chargeback backlog.** The ad-hoc cases from the chargeback file are nearly all fraud — they arrive
*because* they were fraud. They are not a sample of anything and they are not part of the programme.

**Northwater.** Their traffic is cleaner than our book overall: card-present history, older accounts,
and they do their own velocity checks upstream. Their contracted rate is well below the rate we see
across all merchants, which is the number the agreement fixes the standard against.

**Marketing panel.** The 97 % in the sales deck is the benchmark case set. It is case-rich by
construction — it was assembled from confirmed cases — and Legal have told us twice not to quote it in
a certificate under clause 3.3.
