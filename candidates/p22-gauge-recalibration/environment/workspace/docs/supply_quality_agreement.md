# Supply quality agreement — bar stock (extract)

Between Kelvin Works and Brendale Special Steels. Schedule 3, revision 4.

## 3.1 Material specification

Bar stock shall be supplied to the grade and heat-treatment condition stated on the purchase order, with a
material test certificate per heat (recorded in `heats.cert_no`). Hardness shall be reported in HV.

## 3.2 Nonconformance and escalation

Where Kelvin Works considers that product nonconformance is attributable to the supplied material, it shall
raise a supplier nonconformance report.

**Escalation to a change of supplier may be pursued only where the nonconforming rate for the affected
feature that is attributable to the supplied material, measured against the drawing's conformance reference
(see QP-07 §3), exceeds 5.5 % over a six-week window.** Below that rate the parties shall proceed under the corrective-action process in Schedule 5 and no
change of supplier arises.

Rates quoted in any escalation shall state the window, the population and the reference used.

## 3.3 Heat traceability

Each delivery carries a heat identifier. Kelvin Works maintains the mapping from heat to production week in
`heats`. Heat families are assigned by the supplier's melt route; a change of family is notified in advance
and is not, of itself, a nonconformance.

## 3.4 Commercial

Liquidated damages for scrap attributable to material are calculated per Schedule 6 and are not affected by
this Schedule.
