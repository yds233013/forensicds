# P31 — ambient grocery fill-rate dispute (defer-or-overturn control)

Third of the three prospective-validation tasks from `research/phase2/HANDOFF_2026-09-23.md`. Scientific
specification: `research/phase3/spec_p31.md`. Evidence register: `research/phase3/register_p31.md`.

**Why this task exists.** Every task in the phase-1 suite begins with a wrong published number, which is a
structural bias an agent can exploit by always disagreeing. P31 is the corrective required by the handoff's
design principle DP3: on two of its four graded extracts the published figure is **correct** and the
escalation is wrong about the metric while being right about a real account-level tail; on one the published
figure is materially wrong through a latent defect in the reporting code; and on one the agreement itself is
unexecuted on a point that decides the £1.8m question, so the honest answer is that the evidence does not
settle it.

**Mechanism.** Two organisations computing different metrics under the same name, with the contract
adjudicating; plus a correct aggregate coexisting with a genuine tail; plus an unagreed schedule that makes one
consequence non-identifiable.

**What is graded.** Both published figures reproduced; the contractual figure and the range the unexecuted
schedule admits; a four-step bridge between the definitions; the account-level tail; the ticket reconciliation;
and three adjudications — whether the incumbent figure governs, whether the bonus gate is met, and whether the
claim is payable — each of which may be `not_determinable`.

**Anti-pattern insurance.** Always overturning fails on three extracts, always accepting on two, always
deferring on three.
