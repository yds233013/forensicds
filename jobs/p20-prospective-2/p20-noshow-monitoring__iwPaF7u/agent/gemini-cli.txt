I have completed the monitoring review for `noshow-v3.1` in accordance with MRM-04.

### Monitoring Findings

The reported fall in discrimination from 0.77 to 0.71 AUC is an artifact of monitoring the intervened population. Under MRM-04 §4.1, performance must be computed on a population not influenced by the model's use. Evaluating on the **excluded clinics** (CL-001, CL-005, CL-012, CL-028, CL-038) reveals that the model's actual discrimination in service stands at **0.790 AUC**, which is above the validation figure of 0.770 and comfortably exceeds the retention floor of 0.730.

The gap between validation and the vendor's dashboard figure (0.711) decomposes as follows:
*   **Population Drift (+0.007):** Negligible impact from underlying population changes.
*   **Feature Feed Defect (-0.027):** A platform change in week 33 widened the no-show count window to 730 days. While this constitutes a defect (affecting 51% of records), it unintentionally improved discrimination by 2.7 points.
*   **Feature Vintage (+0.014):** Drift in other feature values since booking.
*   **Policy Feedback (+0.066):** The primary cause of the reported decline. The reminder programme reduces no-shows from **21.2% to 10.4%** in the targeted score band, which suppresses the variance of the outcome and artificially lowers the AUC on the intervened population.

### Decision and Action
**Required Action: `retain_model`**

The incumbent `noshow-v3.1` remains within the retention floor on the required evaluation population. Furthermore, the proposed `noshow-v4.0` achieves only **0.764 AUC** on the same clean population—failing the candidate comparison test (§4.4.2) as it is likely overfitted to the intervened distribution.
