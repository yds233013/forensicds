## Conformance reference

Conformance is assessed against the feature's deviation from nominal **as established under QP-07**. Readings
used for disposition must be traceable to the works length standard identified in QP-07 §3.

A part is conforming when the bore deviation from nominal lies within ± 30 µm. Dispositions are recorded in
`inspection_results.disposition`.
