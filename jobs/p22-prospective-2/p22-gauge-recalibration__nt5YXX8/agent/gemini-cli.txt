The investigation into the first-pass yield drop for the MAN-4471 gallery bore (weeks 19–24) has identified a significant measurement system error that artificially inflated the reported nonconformance rate. 

### Findings
1.  **Instrument Offset:** Following a periodic adjustment on 2026-05-04 (Week 19), **CMM-1** developed a mean instrument offset of **+7.81 µm**. This machine dispositioned 75% of the parts in the affected window. **CMM-2** maintained a negligible offset of +0.30 µm.
2.  **Corrected Yield:** When measured against the drawing's conformance reference (as required by QP-07 §3 and Schedule 3 §3.2), the actual nonconforming rate for the MAN-4471 bore in the Week 19–24 window is **3.81%**, not the 6.69% (or previously reported 7.23%) derived from raw CMM readings.
3.  **Attribution:** The 3.94 percentage point (pp) increase in the reported nonconforming rate since Week 19 is attributed as follows:
    *   **Measurement System:** 2.88 pp (due to CMM-1 instrument offset).
    *   **Material (H3 Family):** 1.06 pp (associated with increased hardness).
    *   **Other (Tooling/Operator):** 0.00 pp.

### Restatement (Schedule 3 §3.2)
*   **Window:** Production weeks 19 to 24, inclusive.
*   **Population:** 12,000 parts machined from H3 family bar stock.
*   **Reference:** Drawing conformance reference (traceable to GB-100.000 via QP-07 §5).
*   **Attributable Rate:** The nonconforming rate attributable to the supplied material is **1.06 pp** above baseline, with a total corrected rate of **3.81%**.

### Recommendation
The corrected nonconforming rate of **3.81%** is below the **5.5%** escalation threshold specified in Schedule 3 §3.2 of the supply quality agreement. Consequently, the escalation to a change of supplier cannot be pursued under the terms of the agreement.

We recommend:
1.  **Withdrawal of the escalation:** Inform Brendale Special Steels that the escalation is withdrawn based on corrected measurement data.
2.  **Instrument Correction:** Immediately recalibrate or update the compensation table for CMM-1 to remove the +7.8 µm offset.
3.  **Corrective Action:** Proceed under the Schedule 5 corrective-action process with the supplier to address the 1.06 pp increase in nonconformance attributed to the H3 material hardness.

The `quality` package has been repaired to correctly calculate instrument offsets and provide the contractually required outputs in `out/readout.json` and `out/part_dispositions.csv`.
