# Escalation — ambient grocery service level
From: Commercial Director. To: Head of Demand Science, Category Director, Finance. 2026-07-13.

The service-level report for the quarter claims a **97.5 %** fill rate for ambient grocery. That number is
obviously wrong and I want it rebuilt.

Three of our key accounts have logged shortfall tickets through the quarter. I have Brendale's own quarterly
service report in front of me and it shows **91.6 %** for the same category and the same period. A five-and-a-
half point gap is not a rounding difference. Either our reporting is broken or somebody has chosen a definition
that flatters us.

Two consequences follow if the real number is Brendale's.

First, the demand-science team's bonus gate is written on this metric at 96.0 %, and it should not be paid on a
number the supplier does not recognise.

Second, we have a claim against Brendale worth **£1.8 m** that turns on whether they breached the 95.0 % floor.
On their own figure they plainly did. On ours they plainly did not. I am not prepared to drop a claim of that
size because our own report disagrees with the counterparty.

I want the metric rebuilt from the order lines, and I want to know why our figure and theirs differ. If the
reporting pipeline is producing a number that cannot be reconciled with the supplier's, it needs replacing.
