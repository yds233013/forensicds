### Root Cause of Discrepancy
The gap between Meridian's 97.65% and Brendale's 91.51% (a **-6.14 pp** difference) is fully explained by the differing methodologies:
*   **Denominator (Requested vs. Confirmed): -6.43 pp.** Brendale measures against what was *requested*; the contract permits measurement against what they *confirmed*. This is the largest factor, as Brendale frequently confirms less than requested.
*   **Aggregation (Line vs. Case): +1.83 pp.** Moving from line-based fill to unit-based case-fill actually improves the figure.
*   **Returns Treatment: -1.52 pp.** Brendale deducts returns from their performance; the contract (§7.4) explicitly excludes returns from the fill-rate metric.
*   **Date Window: -0.02 pp.** Negligible impact from using despatch date vs. requested delivery date.
