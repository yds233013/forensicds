# Customer supply agreement — ambient grocery (extract)

Between Meridian Retail Group ("MRG") and its supply base. Schedule 2 (Service), revision 7.

## 7. Service measurement

### 7.1 Fill rate

"Fill rate" means, for a reporting period, the proportion of **order lines** in that period that were filled.

A line is **filled** where the quantity delivered is not less than the **confirmed quantity** for that line.

### 7.2 Confirmed quantity

The confirmed quantity is the quantity the supplier confirmed against the line in response to the order. Where
the supplier confirmed a quantity below the quantity requested, it is the confirmed quantity, not the requested
quantity, against which delivery is measured.

Where **no confirmation is recorded** against a line, the line shall be treated as **not filled**. A line
without a confirmation cannot be shown to have been filled and shall not be excluded from the measurement.

### 7.3 Reporting period

A line falls in the reporting period by its **requested delivery date**. Despatch date is not used for period
assignment.

### 7.4 Exclusions

Lines **cancelled by the customer** before despatch are excluded from both numerator and denominator.

**Returns and rejections are not a fill-rate event.** A line delivered in full and subsequently returned or
rejected remains filled for the purposes of §7.1; returns are dealt with under Schedule 5 (Quality) and are
recoverable separately.

### 7.5 Account floor

The supplier shall maintain a fill rate of not less than **95.0 %** for each account. Where the fill rate for
an account falls below that figure, Schedule 5 remedies apply.

### 7.6 Category figure

Where a figure is quoted for a category it shall be computed on §7.1 over all accounts in that category, on the
period defined in §7.3.

## Schedule 4 — Amendments after confirmation

Where the customer amends the quantity on a line **after** the supplier has confirmed it, the amended quantity
shall be recorded against the line.

**[To be agreed]** The basis on which an amended line is measured for the purposes of Schedule 2 §7.1 — the
quantity originally confirmed, or the amended quantity — remains to be agreed between the parties.
