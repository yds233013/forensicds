# Brendale Ambient — service report, methodology appendix

Issued with the quarterly service report. Reproduced as supplied.

## A1. What we measure

Our service figure is a **case fill**: the proportion of **units ordered** that we delivered.

    case fill = units delivered (net of returns) / units ordered

## A2. Units ordered

Units ordered is the quantity on the customer's purchase order line as received by us — the **requested**
quantity. We do not measure against our own confirmation, because a confirmation is our commitment and
measuring against it would mean we could never fall short of it.

## A3. Returns

Where goods are returned or rejected, the returned quantity is deducted from units delivered. We regard a
returned case as not having served the customer.

## A4. Period

Lines are assigned to the period in which they were **despatched** from our site.

## A5. Scope

All lines on all orders received in the period, excluding lines the customer cancelled.
