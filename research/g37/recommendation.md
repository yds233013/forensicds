# G37 recommendation — **C. DROP** (measurement-system change, as scoped)

## Evidence
- **All four deep-round concepts fail the pre-registered tolerance window** (ratios 0.00–0.01).
  C1, the strongest, fails the counterexample gate in its realistic designs (D1, D2) and in an
  unrealistic stress probe (D3, ratio 0.03).
- **The failure is structural.** In errors-in-variables problems, the quantity that makes
  second-order mistakes material also inflates legitimate-estimator uncertainty. The first-order
  corrections (deconvolve, EIV slope, reject the vendor certificate, overall σ) are separable. The
  many second-order formula variants are not: divisor, instrument, rescale order, δ,
  orthogonal/geometric regression, offset-mean. The rules forbid excluding them.
- **The recognition-vs-execution gate fails.** Once told "both gauges are noisy", an analyst's
  numerically visible remaining work collapses to Deming plus one variance subtraction.
- **Gates that pass:** natural path, cheap-solve, correct-table, graded-fact evidence, IGQA
  semantics, distinctness, threshold provenance. The concept is well-posed and new. It is just not
  *sharply gradable*.

## Why not REDESIGN
Three designs were tried without targeting any method (D1 → D2 → D3 frontier), and none moved the
ratio above 0.03. No principled redesign lever remains within this scope.

## Recorded, not recommended
- **Nonlinear business quantities**, e.g. latent tail fraction / ppm, where deconvolution changes the
  answer by a large multiplicative factor. This is the only untested variant. It needs a
  distributional assumption the analyst cannot fully verify, so it risks F8-type semantic disputes.
  It would need a fresh research round, and it is not endorsed here.
- The G37 slot should go to a different failure mode from the post-G36 tournament (D2 immortal time
  or D3 regression-to-the-mean). Each would need the full gate suite, **starting with the
  identifiability window**, before any design work.

**No candidate was built.**
