# Fixture-coverage prospects

Regimes used (5 per concept) are genuine business variation:
- demand above / below commitments;
- tight capacity;
- urgent orders;
- multi-cavity tooling;
- store openings;
- mix shifts.

**Coverage.** Most important wrong objects are reliably rejected in only **one** regime, the one
where their trap is active (see `separation.md`). Examples:
- A1 W01 in "below";
- B3 W08 in "hot_halls";
- C3 W06 in "many_new_stores".

Meeting "≥ 2 regimes" would require regimes built so that each trap is active, **i.e. fixtures
created to kill specific methods**, which is prohibited. Coverage prospects are therefore poor for
all 12.
