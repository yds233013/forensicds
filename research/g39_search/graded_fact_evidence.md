# Graded-fact evidence

Every candidate object is, by construction, a deterministic functional of visible evidence:
contracts, receiving records, POs, routings, hourly usage, UPS inventories, SCADA/COD records,
step records, forecasts/actuals, store master and counts, and evaluation labels plus the forecast
mix.

No generator-only quantity is needed. **This gate passes for all 12.** The failures are separation
and execution.
