## 3. Pre-registration archived at freeze

both must be dated before any exposure to carry evidentiary weight.

* **Expert solve path** — already committed before this freeze: `research/phase3/spec_p22.md`, `spec_p20.md`,
  `spec_p31.md` (written before any code), plus the `solution_explanation` field of each `task.toml` and the
  intended and unintended routes in each `register_*.md`.
* **Pre-registered analysis plan** — `research/phase3/analysis_plan.md`, written at freeze. It fixes the exposure
  design, the invalid-trial rule applied **before any reasoning is read**, the per-trial labels and their
  definitions, seven predictions each with its stated disconfirming outcome, and the reporting format. It also
  records that nine trials per model family cannot settle any prediction and that the set is sized to detect a
  gross departure only.

## 4. Gates carried forward as BLOCKED

Both were disclosed in the readiness report before authorisation and neither is closed by this freeze.

1. **No adversarial exploit agent was run.** No non-target-model agent is available in this environment. The
   hand-written cheap-solve probes (`tools/p*/exploit_results.txt`) are a weaker substitute and are labelled as
   such.
2. **No independent human re-solve or expert baseline.** Oracle = 1 proves a solution exists; it does not prove a
   competent practitioner reaches the object from the instruction alone. This remains the largest single risk to
   the set, and freezing does not reduce it — it fixes it in place.

One conditional also carried forward: **P20's `hidden_a` sits 0.036 AUC below the retention floor against a
graded tolerance of 0.02**, a 1.8× margin and the thinnest figure in the suite.

## 5. What this freeze forbids from now on

* **No change to any of the three tasks**, for any reason short of a demonstrated verifier defect. A verifier
  defect **excludes the task from aggregates rather than licensing a revision** (the G36 precedent).
* **No change to `analysis_plan.md`**, no re-labelling of a trial once labelled, no tolerance change, and no
  addition or removal of a graded quantity.
* **No task may be shown to a target model before the exposure procedure is authorised.** A task that has been
  shown to a target model outside that procedure becomes a discovery-set task permanently.
